## How to Add or Edit a Product in Swarnix

**In short:** This lets you put a piece of jewellery into your Swarnix catalogue — with photos, weight, price, and who is allowed to see it — so it shows up correctly for your customers on WhatsApp and your website.

**Who can do this:** Owners, Admins, and team members with **Read & Write** access. Team members with **Read Only** access can see the catalogue but won't see the "Add Product" button at all, and the Edit/Delete buttons on each product card are hidden for them too.

**Before you start:** Have a clear photo of the piece ready (plain background, no model in it — this photo is used for image search when customers search by photo on WhatsApp). Know the gross weight and either a fixed price or the metal/purity details if you want the price to update automatically with the daily gold rate.

**Steps:**
1. Go to the **Inventory** tab from the top menu.
2. Tap the gold **"Add Product"** button (top right of the page).
3. Fill in **SKU / Item Code** — this is your own item code, must be unique. It gets capitalised automatically.
4. Fill in **Item Name**, then pick a **Category** (Ring, Earring, Necklace, Bangle, etc.) — if your item doesn't fit, choose **"Other / type your own…"** and type it in.
5. Pick **Sub-category** if relevant (optional), and **Metal Purity** (required — e.g. 22K Gold, 925 Silver; or type your own).
6. Optionally fill in **Collection**, **Size / Measurement**, and pick a **Color** chip (Yellow Gold, Rose Gold, White Gold, Two-Tone, Silver, or tap **"+ Custom"** to name your own).
7. If the piece has diamonds or stones, open the **Stone & Diamond Details** section and fill in what's relevant — Diamond Purity, Color, Weight, Cut, Shape, count, or Stone/Material details. All optional.
8. Enter the **Gross Weight (grams)** — this is required. Net Metal Weight and Stock Quantity are optional.
9. Set **Visible To** — this controls which customers can see this product. Choose **"All Customers"**, **"VVIP Only"**, or **"VVIP & VIP Only"**. Default is All Customers.
10. Choose your pricing: either type a fixed **Price (₹ ex-GST)**, or tap **"⚡ Dynamic Pricing"** to have the price auto-calculate and update every day from the live gold/silver rate (you'll need to fill in metal weight and purity for this).
11. Add a **Description** if you want (craftsmanship notes, certificate numbers, etc.) — optional.
12. If the piece is BIS hallmarked, open **Certificates & Compliance** and add the **HUID** (6-character code) and upload the hallmark certificate photo. Add diamond certificate details too if you have them.
13. Scroll to **Product Images** and upload up to 5 photos — tap **"Upload"** to pick from your gallery, or **"📷 Camera"** to take one directly. The first photo (slot 1) is your primary photo — keep it a plain, jewellery-only shot. At least one photo is required.
14. (Optional) Open **AI Model Try-On** to generate a photo of a model wearing this piece — see the separate SOP on generating an AI model image for the full steps.
15. Tick **"Mark as currently in stock"** if the item is available to sell right now.
16. (Optional) Open the **Variants** section if this design comes in other carat or colour options — see the separate SOP on adding variants.
17. Tap **Save**. Your product now appears in the Inventory list.

To edit later: open **Inventory**, find the product card, tap the pencil/edit icon, change what you need, and save again.

**Good to know:**
- You need at least one photo, an SKU, item name, category, metal purity, gross weight, and a price (or dynamic pricing metal details) before Swarnix will let you save.
- Each photo can be max 5 MB. You get 5 photo slots per product.
- Your SKU has to be unique — if you reuse one, Swarnix will tell you "SKU already exists — please use a different one."
- Deleting a product is a soft delete — your sales history stays safe, the item just stops showing in your live catalogue. There's no undo from the screen though, so double check before confirming.
- Your plan has a limit on total products and total photo storage. If you hit either limit, Swarnix will show an upgrade message instead of letting you add more.

**Common questions:**
- **Can my customer see this product or not?** That depends on what you picked under "Visible To" — All Customers means everyone sees it, VVIP Only means only your VVIP-tier customers see it, and VVIP & VIP Only means both those tiers see it but Regular customers won't.
- **Why can't my staff add or edit products?** Only team members with Read & Write access (or higher — Admin) can add, edit, or delete. If someone has Read Only access, they can browse the catalogue but the action buttons won't appear for them.
- **What's the difference between Stock Quantity and "Mark as currently in stock"?** Stock Quantity is just a number you can track for your own count. "Mark as currently in stock" is the actual toggle that controls whether it shows as available to sell.
- **Do I have to use dynamic pricing?** No — fixed price is the default. Dynamic pricing is only useful if you want the price to auto-update daily with the gold/silver rate instead of you updating it manually.
- **What happens if I delete a product by mistake?** It's removed from your live catalogue immediately and there's no "Cancel" after confirming, so always read the confirmation message — "Delete this product? This cannot be undone" — before tapping Delete.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **"SKU already exists" error** — change the SKU to something unique; Swarnix checks this automatically as you type.
- **Can't save because of a missing field** — Swarnix will pop up exactly which field is missing (e.g. "Weight is required"). Fill that in and try again.
- **Image won't upload** — check the file is under 5 MB and is a real image file (JPEG, PNG, or WebP).

## How to Upload Photos and Generate an AI Model Photo for a Product in Swarnix

**In short:** This lets you upload real photos of a piece of jewellery and also generate a realistic photo of a model wearing it, using AI — handy when you don't have a model shoot budget.

**Who can do this:** Owners, Admins, and team members with **Read & Write** access (same access level needed to add or edit products, since this lives inside the product form).

**Before you start:** Have your product open in **Add/Edit Product** mode, and a clear photo of just the jewellery piece ready (plain background works best). This feature needs the Professional plan or higher — check with the team if you're not sure what plan your store is on.

**Steps — uploading regular product photos:**
1. While adding or editing a product, scroll to the **Product Images** section.
2. Tap **"Upload"** on any of the 5 image slots to pick a photo from your phone or computer, or tap **"📷 Camera"** to take one right there.
3. Repeat for up to 5 photos. Slot 1 is your **Primary** photo — make sure it's a plain, jewellery-only shot, since this is the one used when customers search by photo on WhatsApp.
4. Each photo must be under 5 MB.

**Steps — generating an AI model photo:**
1. In the same product form, open the **AI Model Try-On** section.
2. Either drag a clear jewellery photo into the box that says **"Click or drag jewellery photo here"**, or tap the **"📷 Camera"** button to take one.
3. Tap **"⨳ Generate AI Model"**.
4. Wait roughly 30–60 seconds — you'll see a spinner saying "Generating…".
5. Once ready, you'll see the generated model photo in a preview box. If you don't like it, tap **"🔄 Regenerate"** to try again.
6. Happy with it? Tap **"➕ Add to Images"** — this drops the AI photo straight into your product's photo slots. You'll see a small **"✓ Added to images"** confirmation.
7. Save the product as usual — the AI photo is now part of your product's images.
8. Want to share the photo elsewhere first? Tap **"Share"** to send it via WhatsApp, Gmail, Instagram, Twitter, or just copy the link.

**Good to know:**
- AI model generation only works if your plan includes this feature (Professional plan and above). If your store is on Trial or Starter, ask the team about upgrading.
- There's a monthly limit on how many AI model photos you can generate — if you hit it, Swarnix will show an upgrade message instead of generating more.
- The AI picks the right jewellery type (ring, necklace, earrings, etc.) automatically based on the product's Category, so make sure Category is set correctly before generating.
- The generated photo only becomes part of the product once you tap "Add to Images" — generating it alone doesn't save it.

**Common questions:**
- **Will this replace my real product photos?** No — it adds to your existing photo slots, it doesn't remove your real photos.
- **Can I generate more than one AI model photo for the same product?** Yes, tap "Regenerate" as many times as you like (subject to your monthly limit), and add whichever one you prefer.
- **Why is the Generate button greyed out / not working?** You've likely hit your plan's monthly AI model limit, or your plan doesn't include this feature.
- **Does the AI model photo show the real jewellery or a recreated version?** It's AI-generated based on your uploaded photo, so check it carefully before adding it — it should closely match, but always review it.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **Generation seems stuck or fails** — check your internet connection and try again; if it keeps failing, the monthly limit may be reached.
- **Photo upload rejected** — make sure the file is an actual image (JPEG/PNG/WebP) and under 5 MB.
- **Wrong jewellery type detected** — double check the product's Category is set correctly, since that's what tells the AI what kind of jewellery it's drawing.

## How to Add Product Variants (Different Colour or Carat) in Swarnix

**In short:** This lets you offer the same jewellery design in multiple carat or colour options — like the same ring in 18K Yellow Gold, 18K Rose Gold, and 14K White Gold — all under one product listing, each with its own photos if you want.

**Who can do this:** Owners, Admins, and team members with **Read & Write** access.

**Before you start:** Have the base product already created (or open it now), and know the different carat/colour combinations you want to offer, along with each one's weight and price (or dynamic pricing details).

**Steps:**
1. Open the product in **Add/Edit Product** mode.
2. Scroll down to the **Variants** section.
3. Tap **"➕ Add variant"** — a new variant row appears, collapsed into a summary bar.
4. Tap the summary bar to expand it and fill in the details.
5. Pick the **Metal Purity / Carat** for this variant (e.g. 18K, 22K).
6. Pick a **Color** chip (Yellow Gold, Rose Gold, White Gold, Two-Tone, Silver) or tap **"+ Custom"** for your own colour name.
7. (Optional) Upload up to 5 **variant-specific photos** — tap Upload or use the camera button on each slot. If you skip this, customers will just see the main product's photos for this variant.
8. Fill in any other details that differ for this variant — Diamond Purity, Diamond Color, Diamond Weight, Size, Net Metal Weight, HUID — all optional.
9. Set the **Price** for this variant (fixed) or switch to **Dynamic Pricing** and fill in the metal weight/purity breakdown.
10. Tick **"Mark as currently in stock"** if this specific variant is available right now.
11. Repeat steps 3–10 for each additional variant you want to add.
12. Tap **Save** on the product — all variants are saved together with the product.

**Good to know:**
- If you don't upload separate photos for a variant, Swarnix automatically shows your main product photos instead — so a variant is never left without any photo.
- Removing a variant doesn't delete it permanently right away — it's marked inactive in the background, similar to how deleting a product works.
- Each variant can have totally different pricing (fixed or dynamic) from the main product and from other variants.

**Common questions:**
- **Do I need to upload separate photos for every colour variant?** No — only if the variant genuinely looks different. If photos are skipped, your customer just sees the main product photos.
- **Can each variant have a different price?** Yes, completely independent pricing per variant — fixed price or dynamic pricing, your choice.
- **What if I want to remove a variant later?** Open the product, go to Variants, and remove the one you no longer want, then save.
- **Is there a limit to how many variants I can add to one product?** There's no hard limit mentioned in the app — add as many carat/colour combinations as you genuinely sell.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **Variant won't save** — make sure required pricing info is filled (either a fixed price or full dynamic pricing details).
- **Variant photo missing on storefront** — check whether you actually uploaded a photo for that variant; if not, it should fall back to the main product photo automatically.

## How to Set Product Visibility (All Customers / VVIP / VIP & VVIP) in Swarnix

**In short:** This lets you decide which group of customers is allowed to see a particular product — handy for showing your most exclusive pieces only to your top-tier customers.

**Who can do this:** Owners, Admins, and team members with **Read & Write** access (this setting lives inside the Add/Edit Product form).

**Before you start:** Decide which customer tier should see this product. Note: customer tiers (Regular/VIP/VVIP) are only available as a feature on the Professional plan and above — if your store is on Trial or Starter, every customer is treated the same and this setting won't make a practical difference yet.

**Steps:**
1. Open the product in **Add/Edit Product** mode (Inventory tab → tap a product → edit, or while adding a new one).
2. Find the **"Visible To"** dropdown (it's in the Weights & Stock section).
3. Choose one of three options:
   - **All Customers** — everyone can see this product (this is the default).
   - **VVIP Only** — only customers tagged as VVIP can see it.
   - **VVIP & VIP Only** — customers tagged VVIP or VIP can see it; Regular customers cannot.
4. Tap **Save**.

**Good to know:**
- This is a single choice per product — you can't mix and match beyond these three options.
- The default for every new product is "All Customers," so if you want to restrict something, you have to remember to change it.
- This setting only really matters once you've assigned tiers to your customers — if everyone in your customer list is "Regular," then VVIP-only and VVIP & VIP products simply won't show to anyone yet.

**Common questions:**
- **Can my customer see this product or not?** It depends entirely on what tier you assigned them and what visibility you set on the product — match the two up to know who sees what.
- **Can I set visibility for a whole category at once instead of one product at a time?** No — visibility is set per product, one at a time, inside that product's own form.
- **What happens to a VVIP-only product if I haven't tagged any customers as VVIP yet?** It simply won't be visible to anyone until you assign that tier to at least one customer.
- **Does this affect variants too, or just the main product?** Visibility is a product-level setting — it isn't a separate field per variant.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **Customer says they can't see a product you think they should** — double-check their assigned tier on the Customers page matches the product's Visible To setting.
- **Visibility option didn't save** — make sure you tapped Save on the product form after changing it, not just closed the form.

**Note for the team:** The admin app stores and lets you set the "Visible To" value per product, but in this codebase we found no filtering logic in the dashboard itself that hides products from customers by tier — that enforcement most likely happens in the WhatsApp/website-facing systems outside this admin app. Worth confirming this is wired up end-to-end before relying on it for VVIP-only launches.

## How to Add, Edit, or Delete a Customer in Swarnix

**In short:** This is where you keep your customer list — their contact details and a tier (Regular, VIP, or VVIP) that controls what they can see and use.

**Who can do this:** Owners, Admins, and team members with **Read & Write** access can add, edit, and delete customers. Team members with **Read Only** access can view the customer list and search it, but won't see the Add/Edit/Delete buttons.

**Before you start:** Have the customer's name and WhatsApp number ready. Tier assignment only appears as a field if your store is on the Professional plan or above.

**Steps — adding a customer:**
1. Go to the **Customers** tab from the top menu.
2. Tap the gold **"Add Customer"** button.
3. Type in their **Full Name** (required).
4. If your plan supports tiers, pick a **Customer Tier**: **Regular**, **VIP**, or **VVIP**. New customers default to Regular if you don't change it.
5. Pick the country code (defaults to 🇮🇳 +91 India) and type their **WhatsApp Number** (required).
6. Optionally fill in **Email** and **City**.
7. Optionally add **Notes** — handy for noting preferred designs, anniversaries, or anything special about them.
8. Tap **Save** — you'll see a confirmation and they're added to your list.

**Steps — editing a customer:**
1. On the Customers tab, find the customer in the list.
2. Tap the pencil/edit icon next to their name.
3. Change whatever needs updating — name, tier, phone, email, city, or notes.
4. Tap **"Update Customer"** to save.

**Steps — deleting a customer:**
1. Find the customer in the list.
2. Tap the trash/delete icon next to their name.
3. Confirm when asked: **"Remove this customer? This cannot be undone."**
4. Once confirmed, they're permanently removed from your list.

**Good to know:**
- There's no limit on how many customers you can add — add as many as you like, regardless of your plan.
- Deleting a customer is permanent — there's no soft-delete or recovery option, so be sure before confirming.
- You can search your customer list by name or WhatsApp number using the search box at the top of the page.
- Customer tiers (Regular/VIP/VVIP) only show up as an option if your store is on the Professional plan or Enterprise plan. On Trial or Starter, every customer is treated the same.

**Common questions:**
- **What does assigning a tier actually do?** It controls which products this customer can see (based on each product's "Visible To" setting) and, separately, whether they're allowed to use the selfie virtual try-on feature.
- **Can I change a customer's tier later?** Yes, anytime — open Edit on their record, change the tier, and save.
- **Why don't I see a Tier option when adding a customer?** Tiers are a feature available from the Professional plan upward. If your store is on Trial or Starter, this field won't appear.
- **Why can't my staff member add or remove customers?** Only Read & Write access (or Admin/Owner) can add, edit, or delete. Read Only staff can view and search, but the action buttons are hidden for them.
- **If I delete a customer, can I undo it?** No — deletion is permanent and immediate, so double-check before confirming.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **Can't find a customer in the list** — try the search box at the top (it searches by name or WhatsApp number); also check you didn't accidentally delete them earlier.
- **Tier field missing on the form** — this means your store's current plan doesn't include customer tiers; you'd need the Professional plan or above.
- **Delete failed with an error** — try again; if it keeps failing, there may be a connection issue, so check your internet and retry.

**Note for the team:** The selfie virtual try-on access level (which tiers are allowed to use it) is actually set on the **My Profile & Plan** page, not on individual customers — it's a single store-wide setting with three choices (VVIP customers only / VIP & VVIP customers / All customers), editable by Admins and Owners. Worth keeping this in mind since the brief framed it as tier-controlled per customer, but in the code it's a single store-level switch that then checks against each customer's tier.

## How to View Analytics and Customer Conversation Summaries in Swarnix

**In short:** This is where you see how your inventory and customers are shaping up with simple charts, plus AI-written summaries of what your customers have been chatting about with your WhatsApp AI agent.

**Who can do this:** Anyone logged in — Owners, Admins, and all team members (Read & Write and Read Only) can view Analytics. There's no write action here, just viewing.

**Before you start:** Nothing — you can start right away. Just make sure you have some products and customer chats for the charts to have something to show.

**Steps:**
1. Go to the **Analytics** tab from the top menu.
2. At the top, you'll see **"Usage This Month"** cards showing your Credits used, Products count, and (if your plan includes them) AI Model Calls and Virtual Try-Ons used, each against your plan's limit.
3. Scroll down to see your **Inventory Breakdown** charts — Items by Category, Stock Status (in stock vs sold out), By Material, Price Distribution, and Metal Purity Breakdown. These are available on every plan.
4. If your store is on the Professional plan, Enterprise plan, or still on Trial, you'll also see: a Customer Tiers chart (how many Regular/VIP/VVIP customers you have), a 6-month Credit Usage Trend, an AI Model Usage box, and a Top Categories chart.
5. Scroll further to **Customer Chats** — a list of customers grouped by their WhatsApp conversations, with message counts and how recently they messaged. Tap to expand and read the full conversation thread.
6. To see an AI-written wrap-up of a single customer's chats, tap **"Generate AI Summary"** next to their name in Customer Chats.
7. For a broader summary across many customers at once, scroll to the **Conversation Summaries** section, pick a time period (7 days, 14 days, 30 days, or type a custom number of days, up to 30), and tap **"Generate Summary"**. Give it a few moments — it'll show each customer's name, phone number, message count, and an AI-written summary of what they were asking about.

**Good to know:**
- All the numbers and charts refresh automatically each time you open the page — there's no manual refresh needed for the main charts (Customer Chats has a small refresh button if you want to pull the latest).
- The Conversation Summaries and Customer Chats sections are only available on the Professional plan, Enterprise plan, or while you're still on Trial — Starter plan stores will see an upgrade message instead.
- These conversation summaries currently only cover your **WhatsApp AI agent** chats — there's no separate summary feed for your Web AI agent (website chatbot) conversations at the moment.
- There's no export-to-Excel or download button on this page yet — it's for viewing only.

**Common questions:**
- **Can my staff see Analytics?** Yes — anyone logged in (Owner, Admin, Read & Write, or Read Only) can view this page; it's read-only information for everyone, including Read Only staff.
- **Why don't I see the Customer Chats or Conversation Summaries section?** Your plan might be Starter — these sections need Professional, Enterprise, or Trial.
- **Does this show my website chatbot conversations too, or just WhatsApp?** Right now, all the conversation data and summaries here are from your WhatsApp AI agent only.
- **How far back can I see conversation summaries?** Up to 30 days at a time — pick 7, 14, or 30 days, or type a custom number within that range.
- **Can I download these charts or summaries as a report?** Not currently — there's no export or download option on this page.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **Summary generation seems to hang or fails** — try again after a minute; if it keeps failing, the summary service may be temporarily down.
- **Charts look empty** — this usually just means you don't have enough products or customer chat history yet for that particular chart to show data.
- **Upgrade message instead of Customer Chats** — that section needs Professional plan or above; Starter plan doesn't include it.

**Note for the team:** The brief asked for summaries of both "WhatsApp agent and Web AI agent" conversations, but the code currently only pulls data from WhatsApp conversation logs — there's no Web AI agent / website chatbot data feeding into Analytics yet. Worth confirming whether that's planned or whether the website chatbot logs need to be wired into this page.

## How to Manage Your Team in Swarnix

**In short:** This is where you add staff or family members who need to log into Swarnix on your behalf, and decide exactly what each person is allowed to do.

**Who can do this:** Only the **Owner** (the person who signed up with Google) and team members with **Admin** access can add, edit, or remove team members. Read & Write and Read Only team members cannot manage the team at all — if they open this page, they'll see a message saying only the owner or admin can manage team members.

**Before you start:** Decide on a username and a password (at least 6 characters) for the new person, and which access level fits what they should be allowed to do.

**Steps — adding a team member:**
1. Go to the **Team** tab from the top menu.
2. Tap the gold **"+ Add User"** button.
3. Type their **Full Name** (e.g. "Ramesh Kumar").
4. Pick a **Username** for them (e.g. "ramesh") — this gets saved in lowercase and **cannot be changed later**, so choose carefully.
5. Set a **Password** for them — minimum 6 characters. They'll use this (with their username) to log in.
6. Choose their **Role** by tapping one of the role cards:
   - **Admin** — full access: add/edit/delete products and customers, manage store settings, and manage other team members.
   - **Read & Write** — can add and edit products, customers, and similar day-to-day records, but cannot manage store settings or other team members.
   - **Read Only** — can view everything in the app but cannot add, edit, or delete anything.
7. Optionally add a **Note** (e.g. "Manager at Bandra branch").
8. Tap **"Create User"**.

Tell the new team member their username and password — they'll use the **Staff Login** tab on the Swarnix login screen (separate from the Owner's Google sign-in) to log in.

**Steps — editing a team member:**
1. On the Team tab, find their entry in the list.
2. Tap the pencil/edit icon.
3. You can change their Full Name, Password (leave blank to keep their current one), Role, or Notes. Username cannot be changed.
4. Tap **"Save Changes"**.

**Steps — deactivating or removing a team member:**
1. To temporarily block their access without deleting their record, tap the **Active/Inactive** toggle next to their name — this flips them to Inactive and they can no longer log in, but you can switch them back to Active anytime.
2. To remove them permanently, tap the trash/delete icon next to their name — this deletes them immediately, with no confirmation prompt, so be sure before tapping it.

**Good to know:**
- There's no limit on how many team members you can add, regardless of your plan.
- A username can never be changed once set — if someone needs a new username, you'll have to remove the old account and create a new one.
- Deleting a team member is instant — there's no "are you sure?" pop-up, unlike deleting a product or customer.
- Deactivating (instead of deleting) is the safer option if someone is just on leave or you're not 100% sure you want to remove them yet.

**Common questions:**
- **What exactly can a "Read & Write" team member do that "Read Only" can't?** Read & Write can add and edit things like products and customers. Read Only can only look — no add, edit, or delete buttons show up for them anywhere in the app.
- **Can my Admin staff add other staff members?** Yes — Admin has the same team-management rights as the Owner, including adding, editing, and removing other team members.
- **Can a Read & Write staff member promote themselves to Admin?** No — only existing Owners or Admins can change anyone's role, and Read & Write staff can't even open the Team page to try.
- **If I delete a team member, can I get their account back?** No — deletion is immediate and permanent. If you're not sure, deactivate instead, which you can reverse anytime.
- **Do actions taken by my staff show up as them or as me?** The app doesn't keep a separate log showing which specific staff member did what — all their actions happen under your store's account.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **Forgot a team member's password** — open Edit on their account, type a new password, and save; their old one stops working immediately.
- **Accidentally deleted the wrong team member** — there's no undo, so you'll need to create their account again from scratch with a new username.
- **Team member can't log in** — check they're using the Staff Login tab (not Owner Login), their username is typed correctly (it's case-insensitive), and check their account hasn't been set to Inactive.

## How to Add an Offer in Swarnix

**In short:** This lets you post a seasonal deal or promotion — with a photo or video, details, and an expiry date — that your customers can see.

**Who can do this:** Technically the Add/Edit/Delete buttons are visible to everyone who can open the Offers tab, but only the store Owner's account (and team members sharing that same store login) can actually save changes — Swarnix's backend only allows the store's own account to write offers.

**Before you start:** Have your offer image or video ready (image up to 5 MB, video up to 50 MB), a short catchy title, and know how long the offer should run.

**Steps:**
1. Go to the **Offers** tab from the top menu.
2. Tap the **"Add Offer"** button.
3. Drag and drop your image or video into the upload box, or tap to browse and pick one from your device.
4. Type your **Offer title** (required, up to 120 characters).
5. Optionally add **Details** describing the offer (up to 600 characters).
6. Optionally set **Valid till** — pick a date from the calendar. You can't pick a date in the past. If you leave this blank, the offer simply has no expiry shown.
7. Tap Save — your offer is now posted.

**Steps — editing or deleting an offer:**
1. Find the offer card in your Offers list.
2. Tap the pencil icon to edit — change the image/video, title, details, or validity date, then tap **"Save Changes"**.
3. Tap the trash icon to delete — confirm "Delete this offer?" when asked, and it's removed immediately.

**Good to know:**
- Images must be under 5 MB, videos under 50 MB — both are accepted, this isn't just an image-only feature.
- There's no limit on how many offers you can have running at once.
- Once an offer's "Valid till" date passes, it doesn't disappear from your Offers list — it stays visible to you but gets a dimmed look and an "Expired" label, so you know at a glance it's no longer current.

**Common questions:**
- **What happens to an offer once it expires?** In your Offers list, it just turns slightly faded and gets an "Expired" tag — it isn't deleted, so you can still see your past offers or reuse the details for a new one.
- **Do I have to set an expiry date?** No — it's optional. If you don't set one, the offer simply has no "valid till" shown.
- **Can I upload a video instead of a photo?** Yes — both are supported, video up to 50 MB.
- **Can my staff add offers too?** The Add/Edit/Delete buttons show for anyone who can open the Offers tab, but only your store's own login can actually save the changes — so practically, this works the same as your other staff permissions.
- **Will customers stop seeing an expired offer automatically?** That's the intent, but see the note below — please confirm this with the team.

**If something goes wrong:**

## How to Set Your Own Metal Rates (Override the Daily IBJA Rate) in Swarnix

**In short:** The Metal Rates card shows today's live IBJA gold, silver, and platinum rates. As the jeweller, you can override the gold and silver rates with your own rate or add a premium over IBJA — and Swarnix always prices at whichever is higher, so you never sell below market.

**Who can do this:** Only the store **Owner** (the Google-authenticated login) can edit rates — staff logins can see the rates but the "Edit my rates" button won't appear for them. This is because the override records are tied to the Owner's secure identity.

**Before you start:** Know the rates you want to charge (per 10g for gold, per 1kg for silver) or the premium percentage you want to add over IBJA. Only gold and silver can be overridden — platinum stays IBJA-only.

**Steps:**
1. On your Dashboard, find the **"Today's Metal Rates"** card (it shows the IBJA rates and their date).
2. Tap **"Edit my rates"** (Owner only).
3. For each gold/silver purity, either type your own rate in the **"My rate (₹)"** column, or enter a **"Premium %"** over IBJA — or both.
4. Set **"Auto-switch to IBJA + premium if my rate is older than ___ days"** (1–30) — this protects you if you forget to refresh a rate.
5. Tap **"Save & re-price."** Swarnix saves your rates and re-prices your dynamic-priced products in the background within a few seconds.
6. Overridden purities show a small **"My rate"** tag on the card.

**How the effective rate is chosen:**
- If you entered your own rate recently (within your stale-days window): Swarnix prices at the **higher** of your rate and today's IBJA (so an accidentally-low rate never drags you below market).
- If you only set a premium %: price = IBJA × (1 + premium%).
- If your typed rate has gone stale (older than your chosen days) and you set a premium: it auto-switches to IBJA + your premium, protecting you from an old low number.
- If you set nothing: the plain IBJA rate is used.

**Good to know:**
- Clearing a rate back to empty removes your override for that purity, so pricing reverts to IBJA.
- This directly affects any product using **Dynamic Pricing** — its price recalculates from the effective rate.
- The card caches the last-seen rates so it loads instantly, then refreshes from the database.
- Platinum and other metals can't be overridden — only gold and silver.

**Common questions:**
- **Why is my product still priced at IBJA even though I typed a lower rate?** Because Swarnix always uses the higher of your rate and IBJA — a below-market rate is ignored in favour of IBJA.
- **What happens if I forget to update my rate for a week?** Once it's older than your stale-days setting, Swarnix switches to IBJA + your premium (if any), so you're not stuck on an old low rate.
- **Can my staff change the rates?** No — only the Owner's own login can edit rates.
- **Do my overrides update prices immediately?** Saving fires a background re-pricing of your dynamic products; it completes within a few seconds.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **"Edit my rates" button missing** — you're likely logged in as staff; only the Owner can edit.
- **Prices didn't change after saving** — only Dynamic Pricing products react to rate changes; fixed-price products don't.
- **Rates card shows an error** — check the daily rates have loaded; the card will retry.

## How to Bulk Import Products from a Spreadsheet (AI Bulk Import) in Swarnix

**In short:** AI Bulk Import lets you upload a spreadsheet of your existing stock and have the AI figure out which of your columns map to Swarnix's product fields — so you can add many products at once instead of one by one.

**Who can do this:** This lives inside Inventory, so it follows Inventory access — team members with **Read & Write** access (or Admin/Owner) can import. Read Only staff won't have it.

**Before you start:** Have your product list ready as a spreadsheet. It should at least have an SKU/item code column; other columns like name, category, weight, purity, price, stock, making charges, and stone values will be mapped if present.

**Steps:**
1. Go to the **Inventory** tab.
2. Tap **"AI Bulk Import."**
3. **Step 1 — Upload:** drop in or choose your spreadsheet file.
4. **Step 2 — Map Columns:** the AI suggests how each of your columns maps to Swarnix fields (SKU/HUID, Item Name, Category, Weight, Purity, Price, Stock Qty, Making Charge Type/Value, Stone/Diamond Value, Wastage %, etc.). Review and correct any mapping. SKU is required.
5. **Step 3 — Preview & Import:** check the preview of how the products will come in, then confirm to import them into your catalogue.
6. When done, your Inventory list refreshes with the new products.

**Good to know:**
- The only strictly required field is SKU — but the more columns you map, the more complete each product is.
- Imported products count toward your plan's product limit, same as manually added ones.
- The AI only suggests the mapping — you stay in control and can fix any column before importing.

**Common questions:**
- **What if my column names don't match Swarnix's?** That's the point of the mapping step — the AI matches them and you adjust anything it got wrong.

- **Will importing overwrite my existing products?** Review the preview before confirming so you know exactly what's being added.
- **Can Read Only staff import?** No — importing needs Read & Write access.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **Import blocked at the end** — you may have hit your product limit; check your plan usage.
- **A column mapped wrong** — go back to the Map Columns step and fix it before importing.
- **File won't upload** — make sure it's a supported spreadsheet file.

## How to Send a WhatsApp Marketing Campaign to Your Customers in Swarnix

**In short:** Marketing lets you broadcast an approved WhatsApp template message to a group of your customers — filtered by tier — straight from Swarnix, and see the delivery results.

**Who can do this:** Sending a campaign needs **Read & Write** access (or Admin/Owner). This feature requires a plan that includes Marketing Messages, and your store's WhatsApp Business must be connected.

**Before you start:** Connect your WhatsApp Business number first (Profile page). Have at least one approved WhatsApp message template in your WhatsApp Business account, and decide which customer tier you're targeting.

**Steps:**
1. Open the **Marketing** tab.
2. Refresh/load your approved WhatsApp templates and pick the one you want to send.
3. Give the campaign a **name**.
4. Fill in any template **variables** ({{1}}, {{2}}, …) the template needs.
5. Choose who receives it by **tier**: VVIP only, VVIP & VIP, or All customers. Swarnix shows how many customers match.
6. Review, then confirm to send — you'll be asked to confirm since it messages real customers.
7. Watch the results/logs to see which messages were delivered, are pending, or failed.

**Good to know:**
- You can only send WhatsApp-approved templates — you can't free-type a broadcast, because WhatsApp requires pre-approved templates for business-initiated messages.
- Your WhatsApp Business connection must be active (access token, phone number ID, and WABA ID present).
- Targeting is by customer tier (Regular/VIP/VVIP), so tiers must be assigned for tier filters to be meaningful.
- Sending uses your WhatsApp Business number, so normal WhatsApp messaging rules and any per-message costs apply on WhatsApp's side.

**Common questions:**
- **Why can't I type my own message?** WhatsApp only allows pre-approved templates for outbound campaigns — create/approve templates in your WhatsApp Business account first.
- **Why is the Marketing tab locked?** Either your plan doesn't include Marketing Messages, or WhatsApp Business isn't connected yet.
- **How do I target only my best customers?** Set the tier filter to "VVIP only" or "VVIP & VIP."
- **How do I know if messages went out?** Check the campaign logs — they show delivered, pending, and failed counts.

**If something goes wrong:**
- **No templates appear** — make sure you have approved templates in WhatsApp Business, then refresh.
- **"Not connected" state** — connect WhatsApp Business on the Profile page first (Owner only).
- **Some messages failed** — customers may have invalid numbers or haven't opted in; review the logs for which ones.
